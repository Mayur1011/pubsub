You should reverse this: Do Partitions (Phase 5) first, then Consumer Offsets (Phase 4).
-> Why? In a log-structured message queue (like Kafka), an "offset" is entirely meaningless at the Topic level; it only exists at the Partition level. If you implement consumer offset tracking and consumer groups in Phase 4 based purely on topics, you will have to delete and rewrite almost all of that code in Phase 5 when you split topics into partitions.

By defining a Topic as a collection of Partitions early on (even if a topic defaults to having just 1 partition initially), your data structures will remain stable when you scale up.
